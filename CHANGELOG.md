# Changelog

What's new in each release of Notes. The newest is at the top.

## 1.0.0

- Notes are plain Markdown (.md) files in a folder you choose; no account and no cloud.
- A pinned welcome note with an example of every kind of formatting, added the first time you open a folder.
- Notes show as a grid of cards (or a list), with pinned notes at the top, as in Google Keep.
- Checklists: tick items off, add items with Enter, indent them, and keep ticked items folded away at the bottom. Turn any note into a checklist and back.
- Colours, labels, pins, archive and reminders, kept inside each note's file so they travel with it.
- Pictures from your gallery, saved next to the note and shown across its top.
- Pick several notes with a long press to pin, colour, label, archive or delete them together.
- Browse: reminders, archive, trash, every label and your folders in one place.
- Deleted notes wait in the trash for 7 days, and can be put back. Undo after deleting or archiving.
- Notes save themselves as you type, and update on screen when their file changes on another device or in another app. If a note changes elsewhere while you're editing it, you choose which version to keep.
- A formatted view with headings, lists, tickable checkboxes, quotes, code, pictures and links.
- Search across titles, text and labels, archived notes included.
